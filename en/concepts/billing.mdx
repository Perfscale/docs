## Overview

On the RU deployment (perfscale.ru), upgrading a workspace is self-serve:
pick a plan, pay by bank card or SBP via **YooKassa**, and the plan activates
automatically within a minute — no invoices or sales calls. Your current
plan, paid-until date, and renewal state are shown in **Settings → General**;
payments and the subscription controls live in **Settings → Billing**.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Starter** | 5 000 ₽/mo | Up to 10 machines · 30-day result retention · 10 GB data |
| **Growing** | 25 000 ₽/mo | Up to 100 machines · 90-day retention · 100 GB data · API + CI/CD integrations |
| **Enterprise** | on request | Unlimited machines · up to 1-year retention · 1 TB+ data · on-premise, SSO/SAML |

![The checkout plan picker — Starter, Growing, and Enterprise with payment via YooKassa](/docs-assets/platform/checkout-picker.webp)

Starter and Growing are paid right from the picker. Enterprise is provisioned
manually — use **Contact us** on the same screen.

## Subscription

A successful payment starts or extends a subscription, tracked as two values:

- **Paid until** — the end of the current paid period. The workspace keeps
  full access until that moment.
- **Auto-renew** — whether the subscription renews automatically at the end
  of the paid period.

Cancelling auto-renew refunds nothing: access simply continues until the
paid-until date. On the paid-only RU contour the workspace then loses access
(the API answers `402 Payment Required`) until the next payment — billing and
settings stay reachable so you can pay. You can resume auto-renew at any time
before the period ends.

## Refunds

Refunds are deliberately narrow: only the workspace's **first succeeded
payment**, and only **within 3 days** of it. The money goes back through
YooKassa in full and the paid period ends. After that window the only lever
is cancelling auto-renew.

One refund happens automatically: **upgrading your plan mid-period**. Paying
a higher tier while a lower-tier period is still active activates the higher
tier immediately on a fresh period — and the superseded lower-tier payment is
refunded in full, no request needed.

## Payment history

**Settings → Billing** lists your recent payments (up to 50) with amount,
payment method, status, and refund details where applicable — every charge
and every refund is auditable from the same place.
